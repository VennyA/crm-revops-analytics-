# CRM RevOps Analytics

> **End-to-end Revenue Operations analysis of a synthetic B2B SaaS business — from lead acquisition to customer churn — conducted across Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.**

## Executive Summary

This project simulates a real-world Revenue Operations analyst scenario for a B2B SaaS company operating across five global regions. Using a synthetic dataset of 10,000 leads across seven interconnected tables, the analysis answers 33 business questions covering the full customer lifecycle — from lead acquisition through conversion, revenue generation, and churn.

Key findings revealed that CyberSecurity and HealthTech are the strongest acquisition segments, Enterprise customers generate 63% of total ARR ($43.9M), and poor customer support is the leading driver of churn. Net Revenue Retention stands at 81.69%, indicating the business cannot grow from existing customers alone and must reduce churn to improve long-term sustainability.
